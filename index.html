Hi, I'm Honey Norfolk.

I'm a student researching robotics, artificial intelligence, and the ways intelligent systems affect people and society.

I work across robotics engineering and sociology, with a growing interest in how policy, economics, and ethics shape the systems we build and the communities they affect.

Research interests

Robotics Artificial Intelligence Human-Robot Interaction Technology Policy Ethics Sociology Economics

Research

My work examines intelligent systems from both technical and social perspectives, from human-robot interaction to AI in education and public life.

Projects

Push It (Real Good): Human Choices in Group Interaction

Oregon State University REU · 2025

Studied how people interacted with a social robot during group decision-making scenarios. The study explored how different robot personalities related to participants' choices to engage with the robot.

HRI Social Robotics Group Decisions

Social Robots for Procedural Guidance

Collaborative research project

Examined how a social robot could provide procedural guidance to individuals and groups during routine and time-critical scenarios. Measures included compliance, verification, errors, trust, workload, and situation awareness.

Human-Robot Interaction Trust Safety

AI Can't Write Like That!

University of Central Arkansas · JURA

Investigated how readers respond to writing in different styles and whether they can distinguish human-written, AI-generated, and hybrid text.

AI Writing Sociology

AI Literacy in Education

Education research and outreach

Explored how students understand AI-generated content, academic integrity, and the role of AI in learning. Related work includes AI literacy activities and educational research.

Education AI Literacy Academic Integrity
