About me

I'm an undergraduate researcher studying Robotics Engineering and Sociology at the University of Central Arkansas, with a minor in Economics.

My research focuses on human-robot interaction, artificial intelligence, and the social questions surrounding intelligent systems. I'm interested in how people respond to robots, how these systems shape human decisions and behavior, and how they should be designed and governed.

Through robotics research, sociology, and public service, I explore how technical systems affect people and communities, including those who may have little influence over how these technologies are introduced.

Human Choices to Engage with Social Robots

Examining how robot personality and group dynamics influence people's decisions to interact with a social robot during group decision-making tasks.

Investigating how robots guide people through procedural tasks and how group context, time pressure, and system errors influence human responses.
