// research files

Questions I'm investigating, robots I've bothered, and things I've learned along the way.

HRI / GROUP INTERACTION

How do people choose to interact with social robots?

Research using the Quori robot to explore how people respond to different robot personalities during group decision-making, including a trolley dilemma task.

HRI / PROCEDURAL GUIDANCE

Robots helping people get things done

A project exploring how social robots can guide people through procedures, including the effects of working alone or in groups and responding to errors or time pressure.

AI / WRITING

AI Can't Write Like That!

A study examining how readers respond to writing across different styles, including academic, newspaper, tabloid, and informal email writing.

AI / EDUCATION

How are educators navigating AI?

Interview-based research examining how educational institutions approach AI policy, teaching practices, academic integrity, and faculty support.
