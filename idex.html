About Me

I'm a Robotics Engineering and Sociology student interested in human-centered robotics, artificial intelligence, and the ways technology shapes society. My work brings together technical research and social questions about how people experience, understand, and live alongside intelligent systems.

I'm especially interested in how robots influence human decisions, how people respond to robots in social settings, and how these systems can be designed and governed responsibly.

Research

Projects exploring people, intelligent systems, and society.

HUMAN-ROBOT INTERACTION · OREGON STATE UNIVERSITY REU

Human Choices in Group Interaction with Social Robots

Investigated how people engage with a social robot during a group moral decision-making task, examining robot personality conditions and participants' choices to interact with the robot or other people.

HRI Social Robotics Human Behavior

SOCIAL ROBOTICS · PROCEDURAL GUIDANCE

Social Robots for Procedural Guidance

Studied how a robot's guidance may affect people completing procedural tasks, including individual and group settings, time pressure, errors, trust, and task performance.

Robotics Trust Human Factors

AI AND WRITING · UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH

“AI Can't Write Like That!”

Examined how writing style relates to readers' ability to distinguish human-written text from AI-generated text across different writing contexts.

Accepted by the University of Central Arkansas Journal for Undergraduate Research and Advocacy (JURA).

Generative AI Authorship Writing

AI IN EDUCATION

AI Use, Teaching, and Academic Integrity

Explored how students and educators navigate AI use, academic integrity, classroom expectations, and institutional support.
